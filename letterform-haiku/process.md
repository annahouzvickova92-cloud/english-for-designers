The Process of Making My Letterform

The Idea
I wanted to create a letterform using a typical Czech material. I chose Olomoucké tvarůžky, 
a traditional Czech chesee known for its strong smell and distinctive appearance.

Making the Letterform 
First, I cut Olomoucké tvarůžky into thin slices and placed them in the oven at 180°C.
As the cheese heated up, bubbles started to form on its surface. They expanded and changed shape naturally.
I did not shape the letter by hand. Instead I observed how the cheese transformed in the oven and how the bubbles started to create unexpected shapes.
The final letterform emerged from the material's natural reaction to heat. I found it interesting that I could not fully control the results.
The material became part of the process.
